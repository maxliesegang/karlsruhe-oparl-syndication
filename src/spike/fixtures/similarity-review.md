# Review: ähnliche Vorlagen

Generated by `npm run review:similarity -- --write` from the committed archive.
30 papers dated 2024-01-01 or later, each with its top 5 TF-IDF
neighbours *before* any score threshold, explicitly related papers already excluded.

Replace the blank in each `- [ ]` with one letter:

- `s` — same matter: an earlier or later step, a reply, a follow-up
- `t` — related topic: a reader of the paper above would want this pointed out
- `n` — not useful: same kind of paperwork, a shared word, unrelated
- `?` — evidence or usefulness remains uncertain; excluded from precision

Read the [original-source companion](similarity-review-context.md) for longer excerpts
and links to the original PDFs and extracted text. Regenerate it with
`npm run review:similarity -- --context`: this preserves these pairs and labels.
It deliberately omits scores and proposed labels so the evidence can be read first.

Judge as a reader of the paper above would, not by the score. Shared templates,
authors, budget rounds or legal procedures alone are `n`. The same specific
project or agreement at a different stage is `s`; useful related context is `t`.
Recurring appointments or annual plans are not automatically the same matter.
An empty checkbox means not reviewed; `?` means reviewed but unresolved.

The original 11 human labels are preserved verbatim. Added labels are provisional
assistant judgments based on the committed original text, with confidence and a
reason below each pair. They are not independent human validation. To override
one, change the checkbox, remove its `reviewer: assistant` annotation and replace
the assistant reason with your own. High confidence concerns the judgment,
not the TF-IDF score. Use medium-confidence and `?` pairs as the human spot-check queue.

Run `npm run review:similarity` for results separated by reviewer, score band,
threshold and rank, including unresolved counts and useful-query coverage.
This small, clustered sample is exploratory; do not choose a production threshold
from the assistant labels alone.
